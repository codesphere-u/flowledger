Frequently Asked Questions

Ledger & compliance details

Are provider adapters live or offline?

All provider adapters (M-Pesa Daraja, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Wave, Orange Money, USDC/EURC) build and parse spec-conformant payloads offline with HMAC verification and failure injection.

What does Nexus Aegis G6 export certify?

flowledger_export_aegis_g6 emits canonical G6_FINANCIAL_INTEGRITY RFC 6962 SHA-256 Merkle leaves proving zero-sum ledger balance and chain continuity.
