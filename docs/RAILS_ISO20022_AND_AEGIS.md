# Payment Rails, ISO 20022 & Nexus Aegis G6

## Core Capabilities
- **Pan-African Mobile Money & FX**: Offline Daraja M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Wave, Orange Money, Tigo Pesa, and single-use rate-locked FX corridors across 12 currencies.
- **ISO 20022**: Generate & parse `pacs.008`, `pacs.002`, and `camt.053` XML into integer minor units.
- **MCP & Aegis G6**: Run `flowledger mcp` (12 tools) and `flowledger export-aegis` for RFC 6962 `G6_FINANCIAL_INTEGRITY` attestation.
